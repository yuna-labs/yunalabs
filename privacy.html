Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Yuna Labs collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and in plain language, the same way we work.

Last updated: 6 June 2026

Who we are

Yuna Labs (“we,” “us”) is a data and AI engineering studio based at 30 N Gould St, Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at info@yuna-labs.com.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, optional company name, and the message you send.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically logs standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site secure and running.
  • Aggregate analytics. We may use privacy-respecting, aggregate analytics to understand general site traffic. This helps us improve the site and does not identify you personally.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and discuss a potential project.
  • To provide and improve our services and this website.
  • To keep our site secure and prevent abuse (for example, spam filtering on our form).

We do not sell your information, and we don’t send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We only share information with the service providers that help us operate, for example our website host and our email provider, and only so they can perform those services for us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Any analytics we use are aggregate and do not rely on identifying you.

How long we keep it

We keep contact inquiries only as long as needed to respond and, where relevant, to maintain a record of our correspondence. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Just email info@yuna-labs.com and we’ll take care of it.

Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we’ll revise the “last updated” date above. Significant changes will be made clear on this page.

Questions about your data?

We’re happy to explain anything here in plain language. Reach out any time.
