Engagement Terms

How we work with our clients.

Standard terms governing project engagements between Yuna Labs and its clients.

How we work

We charge by project, not by the hour. You know the total cost upfront, and there are no surprise invoices. The scope, deliverables, and timeline are defined in the Statement of Work before any payment is made.

Payment structure

Our standard project structure is:

  • 10% on signing
  • Remaining 90% distributed across project milestones, tied to deliverables

The exact milestone breakdown depends on project scope and timeline, and is defined in the Statement of Work before signing. Typical structures:

Standard · 4–12 weeks

10 / 30 / 30 / 30

Across four milestones, each tied to a defined deliverable and acceptance criteria.

Small · < 4 weeks

10 / 60 / 30

Three milestones (signing, delivery, and acceptance) for tighter, single-track work.

Large · 3+ months

Custom milestones

Additional milestones to smooth cash flow on both sides without sacrificing accountability.

Each milestone has written acceptance criteria defined in the Statement of Work. A milestone is considered accepted when the client signs off, or automatically after 5 business days if no feedback is provided.

Our guarantee

If at any point before acceptance of the project’s halfway milestone we fail to deliver against the agreed-upon acceptance criteria, and we are unable to remediate the issue within 10 business days, the client is entitled to a 50% refund of all payments made to date.

No arguments, no drawn-out process. We offer this because we’re confident in our team and our process. We’d rather give a clean exit than deliver work the client isn’t happy with.

What the refund covers

The refund applies to fees paid directly to Yuna Labs. Third-party costs (cloud infrastructure, software subscriptions, paid connectors, etc.) are non-refundable, as these are paid to outside vendors.

Upon refund, all work product, deliverables, code, models, and documentation remain the property of Yuna Labs. The engagement ends cleanly with no further obligations on either side.

Scope changes

Material changes to the agreed scope require a written change order with adjusted pricing and timeline, signed by both parties before the new work begins. The guarantee above applies only to the originally scoped work, not to added scope.

Small refinements and normal iteration within the defined scope are expected and included. We’ll be upfront about what counts as a change order versus normal iteration.

What we need from the client

Timely delivery depends on a few things from the client side:

  • Access to required data sources within 5 business days of project kickoff
  • Stakeholder availability for scheduled review sessions
  • Sign-off or written feedback on milestone deliverables within 5 business days of submission

Delays on the client side extend the project timeline proportionally and do not constitute a failure on our part under the guarantee.

After the project

Once the final milestone is accepted, the project is considered complete and all deliverables are owned by the client. Ongoing support, new features, and maintenance are available under a separate retainer agreement at the client’s option.

Frequently asked questions

What does an audit actually produce?

A written assessment, typically 20–40 pages, covering your current stack, data contracts, observability posture, security gaps, and cost. You leave with a prioritized backlog of recommendations ranked by ROI and a costed roadmap if you want to take next steps with us.

The deliverable is yours to keep regardless of whether you continue with us. No pitch deck at the end.

We already use dbt / Snowflake / Looker. Do you build on what we have or rip it out?

We build on it. The point of working with us is that you keep your data, your warehouse, and your existing investments. We add the rigor (tests, lineage, docs, contracts) and fix what’s broken.

We’ll only recommend a replacement if the existing tool is actively causing a problem we can’t solve in place, and we’ll explain exactly why, in writing, before you decide.

Do you work with AWS, Azure, and GCP?

Yes, all three, plus the major warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks). Our work is vendor-neutral on principle: we recommend based on what fits your stack and your team, not on what we have a partnership with.

What happens if a milestone slips?

If the slip is on us, we extend the timeline at no additional cost and the guarantee in the Statement of Work still applies. If it’s caused by something on the client side (data access delays, late sign-off, scope changes), the timeline shifts proportionally.

Either way, you hear from us the moment we know there’s a risk, not at the milestone deadline.

Do you replace our existing team or work alongside them?

Alongside. We’re a senior studio, not a body shop. Our value is in unblocking your team and leaving them with code, documentation, and patterns they can run with after we’re gone.

Every engagement includes pairing sessions and a written handoff. If you don’t want a handoff because you’d rather we run it ongoing, that’s what the retainer is for.

Can you handle sensitive data, PHI, PII, financial records?

Yes. We’ve worked under HIPAA and SOC 2 patterns and can sign a BAA. We do not copy or store your data outside your environment. All work happens against your accounts, in your cloud, with audit trails on every access.

Specific data-handling terms are agreed in the Statement of Work before any access is granted.

Who owns the code after the project ends?

You do. Once the final milestone is accepted, all deliverables (code, models, documentation, dashboards) belong to you. No license fees, no escrow, no lock-in.

The only exception is the 50% refund clause under the guarantee. If that’s triggered, IP stays with us. That’s the trade-off for a clean exit.

How fast can you start?

For audits: typically within 1–2 weeks of signing. For builds: 2–4 weeks, depending on team availability and how quickly we can get access to your data.

We’ll be honest about our pipeline on the discovery call. We’d rather decline a project than overpromise a start date we can’t hit.

Questions about a specific engagement?

We’re happy to walk through the Statement of Work line by line before anything is signed.
