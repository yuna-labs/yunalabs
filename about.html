About Yuna Labs

We’re a data and AI engineering studio. We help companies stop wrestling with their data and start making confident decisions with it, using the rigor of real software engineering, explained in language everyone can follow.

Why we exist

Most teams don’t have a data problem because they lack data. They have too much of it, scattered across too many tools, and no single version they can trust. So reports disagree, dashboards quietly go stale, and big decisions get made on a gut feeling and a spreadsheet someone exported last Tuesday.

We think that data should be engineered with the same care as production software: connected, tested, documented, and observable, so every number can be defended and every change can be reviewed and rolled back safely. Get that foundation right, and good analytics, clear dashboards, and genuinely useful AI follow naturally.

What we believe

Plain language beats jargon

If we can’t explain what we’re building and why, we don’t understand it well enough. Everyone in the room should follow along.

A number is only as good as its source

Every metric has a definition, an owner, and tests. You should always be able to trace a figure back to where it came from.

Build it to last

Versioned, tested, and observable by default, so systems survive growth, staff changes, and the occasional bad day.

You own what we build

Code, documentation, and infrastructure live in your accounts. No lock-in, no black boxes, no hostage situations.

Who you’ll work with

When you hire Yuna Labs, you work directly with the people building your project, with no account managers, no handoffs, and no junior staff learning on your dime. You’ll always know exactly who’s doing the work and why.

We’re a focused studio, so we only take on work we’re confident we can deliver, and we back it with a 50% money-back guarantee. If we don’t deliver what we agreed, you’re never stuck with us.

How we work with you

  • Talk. A free consultation to understand your goals and where data can help most.
  • Plan. A clear scope, cost, and timeline you approve before any work begins.
  • Build & deliver. Small, tested increments with honest weekly updates, and you own it all at the end.

Where we’re based

Yuna Labs is headquartered in Sheridan, Wyoming, and works with teams across North America. Remote-first and async-friendly, built for the way modern teams actually operate.
