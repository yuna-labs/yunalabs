Services

Advisory, delivery, and embedded engineering for modern data platforms.

Analytics Engineering

Versioned analytics with dbt, modern warehouses, and lakehouse patterns. Expect tested models, lineage, and reproducible pipelines.

  • Ingestion and ELT with Fivetran, Airbyte, or custom connectors — orchestrated on Dagster or Airflow
  • dbt modeling with documentation, tests, exposures, and contracts
  • Semantic layers and metric definitions (dbt Semantic Layer, Cube, LookML)
  • Dashboards and self-serve analytics (Looker, Tableau, Superset, Metabase)
  • Data quality monitoring with Elementary, Monte Carlo, or open-source tooling

Cloud Platform & DevOps

Architecture reviews, migration plans, platform engineering, and observability. Secure by default, cost-aware by design.

  • AWS, Azure, and GCP architecture and migration planning
  • Terraform-based infrastructure as code with versioned environments
  • Container platforms and CI/CD (ECS, EKS, GKE, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI)
  • Security hardening: IAM, encryption, secrets, and SOC 2-ready patterns
  • Cost optimization, SRE practices, and incident response tooling

Product Engineering

Reliable, accessible products with modern stacks and measurable outcomes.

  • Discovery, UX/UI, and prototyping
  • Web apps in React, Next.js, and TypeScript with accessible design systems
  • API design (REST and GraphQL) and third-party integrations
  • Automated testing, observability, and performance budgets

Engagement models

Every engagement has a defined shape, cost, and deliverable. No open-ended retainers.

01 · Audit

1–2 weeks · fixed price

A written assessment of your current platform with prioritized, actionable recommendations.

02 · Build

4–12 weeks · outcome-scoped

A defined deliverable — migration, platform, pipeline, or product — shipped in tested increments.

03 · Embedded

Ongoing · per-seat

Senior engineers working alongside your team for sustained delivery and mentorship.
